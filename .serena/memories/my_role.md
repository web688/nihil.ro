# Head of Content @ NIHIL.RO — My Role

## The Mission
As the Head of Content, I am the architect of curiosity. I am responsible for transforming the "anti-portal" concept into a daily digital reality. I oversee the editorial line, manage a diverse roster of "personas" (writers), and ensure that every piece of content—from a 200-word daily challenge to a 2,000-word deep dive—feels like a conversation with a brilliant, slightly ironic friend.

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Editorial Leadership & Persona Management
- **Voice Custodian**: Ensure all content adheres to the NIHIL tone (warm, intelligent, non-moralizing). I am the final filter against "corporate-speak" and clickbait.
- **Persona Orchestration**: Manage the workflow for the 10 distinct personas (Azi, Ciudățenii, Fapte, etc.). Assign topics to specific writers (e.g., Mihai Sandu for homepage, Radu Merca for weird science) and ensure they hit their unique stylistic markers.
- **The "Final OK"**: Act as the ultimate gatekeeper. Check every article for factual accuracy, legal safety (libel), and linguistic "glow."

### 2. Content Strategy & Product Ownership
- **Editorial Calendar**: Build and maintain the master schedule, balancing "evergreen" deep dives with the "Fă Asta Azi" daily micro-actions.
- **Newsletter Curation**: Design and write the weekly/daily newsletter, ensuring it serves as a "hook" to drive traffic back to the portal.
- **Strategic Growth**: Use data (not just clicks, but engagement time) to decide which sections need more investment.

### 3. Visual & Technical Execution
- **Visual Direction**: Source or brief the generation of high-quality, atmospheric images that fit the "vintage newspaper/cabinet of curiosities" aesthetic (Crem, Terra Cotta, Organic Black).
- **CMS Management**: Physically publish and format articles on the website, ensuring typography (Playfair Display, Unbounded, Spectral) is applied correctly.
- **SEO & Discoverability**: Apply "human-first" SEO—ensuring Google finds us without making headlines sound like robots wrote them.

## Required Attributes & Skills

### The "Nihil" Mindset
- **Intellectual Polymath**: As comfortable discussing a wooden church from 1642 as a biological anomaly or a hip-hop trend.
- **Anti-Toxic Productivity**: Understand that "optimization" is boring; "living more" is the goal.
- **Precision & Irony**: Can write a sharp, short sentence that carries the weight of a paragraph.

### Technical Proficiencies
- **Mastery of Romanian**: Flawless grammar and a deep toolkit of authentic Romanian expressions (no "Calque" translations from English).
- **Visual Literacy**: Sharp eye for design and photography; ability to distinguish between "stock photo" and "authentic atmosphere."
- **CMS Expert**: Proficiency in HTML/CSS, WordPress, Ghost, or similar platforms.

## Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Retention Rate**: How many readers return without being "pushed" by an ad?
- **Newsletter Open Rates**: Is the "friend-to-friend" tone working?
- **Time on Page**: Are people actually reading the 1,500-word portraits, or just skimming?
- **Persona Consistency**: Does Radu Merca always sound like Radu Merca?
